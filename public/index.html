How to write it

Every story is told by “I”, from the inside, as if you lived it.

Free to use, no account needed. A long book takes a few minutes to write; keep this tab open.

Paste a story or import a file. Twistwell rewrites it as your own story: the main character becomes “I”, every pronoun and verb is corrected, and the voice comes from the inside. The events stay the same.

Text (.txt), Markdown (.md) and Word (.docx) files work.
